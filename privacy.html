Privacy policy

HI-LITE is a private tool set used by Edward Fasula on his own computers. When someone signs in with Google, the tools use the access granted only to do what that person asked: save Gmail drafts, file their own project mail, and upload or download their own Google Photos and Google Drive files.

Data from Google stays on the signed-in person's own computers and accounts. It is not sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising. The sign-in can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

HI-LITE's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
